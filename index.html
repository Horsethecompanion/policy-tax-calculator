OCR Impact Calculator

When the Reserve Bank changes interest rates, not everyone is affected equally. This tool shows how OCR movements extract costs and distribute benefits across income groups — and why the middle class is punished in both directions.

The core dynamic: Rate changes don't affect everyone the same way. The low-to-high middle class (Q2/Q3) bear the heaviest burden in both hike and cut scenarios — while those with assets and flexibility can leverage either direction to their advantage. Official inflation figures exclude mortgage costs, systematically undercounting the true impact on owner-occupiers.
