Who bears the real cost of interest rate changes?

The Reserve Bank's Official Cash Rate (OCR) doesn't affect everyone equally. Low-income earners are protected by the social contract. High-income earners can shift investments to benefit either way. It's the moderate income middle — too rich for support, too constrained by debt — that bears the heaviest burden.

Your Position in the Curve

Moderate-income earners sit in the most exposed position. Too "wealthy" to receive meaningful government support, but carrying enough debt — or paying enough rent — to feel every OCR move. When rates rise, your mortgage or rent costs climb. When rates fall, housing prices inflate, raising your long-term housing costs.
